# Google Play listing — English

## App name
Candle online | In remembrance

## Short description
Light a candle, share a message and keep someone else’s flame burning.

## Full description
Sometimes a small light helps express what words cannot.

Candle is a place for virtual candles of remembrance, hope and support. Light a candle for someone or a meaningful moment, then share it with the people who matter to you.

Make it yours
Add a message and choose a background image. Return to your candle and tap to keep its flame burning.

Support one another
Visit other people’s candles and extend their burning time. A counter shows how many different people have supported each candle, excluding its author.

Explore the map
Place your candle at a location you choose or discover candles nearby. Use your current location with permission, or move the map manually.

Share a moment
Send a candle link to someone you care about. They can open it in a browser too.

Sign in with Google, Telegram, or a username and password. Choose English, Ukrainian or Russian in settings.

Candles, messages, backgrounds and chosen locations are visible to other users. You can report inappropriate content and block its author.
