# Name and logo

"Candle", "Tap to Burn" and the Candle logo identify the official app and service
run by the Candle maintainers. The code licence ([`LICENSE`](LICENSE)) does not give
any rights to them.

## You may

- Say that your project is based on, or is a fork of, Candle.
- Use the name to refer to the official app, for example in articles, reviews
  and tutorials.

## You may not

- Publish a fork, app, website or service under the name "Candle" or a confusingly
  similar name, or with the Candle logo or app icon.
- Suggest that your project is official or endorsed by the Candle maintainers.

If you publish a fork, give it a different name and icon, and replace the assets
listed in [`LICENSE-ASSETS.md`](LICENSE-ASSETS.md).

Questions: open an issue in the repository.
